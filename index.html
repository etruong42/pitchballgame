How to write notes
  • G4 one beat of G in octave 4 (C4 is middle C). Sharps and flats: F#3, Bb4.
  • G4:2 two beats · G4:0.5 half a beat · G4:1/3 a triplet.
  • R:1 a one-beat rest: a gap with no wall.
  • G4:0.25~ a quick note: heard in the demo, but with no wall to fly through.
  • Separate notes with spaces, commas or new lines. | bar lines and // comments are ignored.
  • Written in is the key you typed the notes in. Picking a song sets Key, Octave and Mode to match, so it plays as written; change them to transpose.
  • Lyrics: one syllable per note, in order. Split words into syllables with hyphens (A-ma-zing), use _ to hold a syllable over another note, and / or a new line to start a new line of the karaoke display.
  • Very short notes are slowed down to about 0.18 s each so the ball has time to move. Use ~ to keep fast runs exact.
